AgentOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

AgentOS is a private automation system operated for personal use by its owner.

Google Drive Access

AgentOS requests read-only access to Google Drive to retrieve documents, file metadata, and project information authorised by its owner.

Data Storage and Use

Retrieved information may be stored temporarily or cached on the owner's private infrastructure to support authorised automation tasks.

AgentOS does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

Access and Security

Access is restricted to the owner and systems authorised by the owner. Credentials are stored separately from retrieved document content.

Data Deletion

The owner controls stored data and can delete local copies and revoke Google Drive access through Google Account permissions.
